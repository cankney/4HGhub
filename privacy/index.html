Privacy Policy for Odoo to SpeedShip Bridge

This extension is an internal productivity tool that transfers shipping address data directly between open Odoo tabs and SpeedShip tabs within the user's browser. It does not collect, store remotely, track, or transmit any user or company data to external servers or third parties. All processing and temporary storage occur strictly on the user's local machine.
